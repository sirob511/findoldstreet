Old German street names in Breslau/Wroclaw
From different sources we tried to make a comprehensive overview of old German street names in Breslau/Wroclaw. We hope that this list is a good addition to our post about
Genealogy in Wroclaw and Lower Silesia
and could help you to find some traces of your family history!
Of course we cannot guarantee that these are the latest street names, as in the last decades street names were changed – but the list should be fairly accurate. If you have further hints then please use the comment box at the bottom of this page!
If you are planning a trip to Wroclaw, then our blog is full of useful resources, no matter if
our recommendations for the best restaurants or cafés where we locals go in Wroclaw
, or maybe our planning guides where you can find
useful information about getting into Wroclaw and some pre-selected accommodations
where we normally put our friends and family if we don‘t have enough space…
