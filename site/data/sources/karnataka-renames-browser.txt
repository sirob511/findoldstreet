L2@P0: Karnataka State, India: Name Changes
L3@P0: Karnataka is a state in South West India. The official language of the state is Kannada1
L4@P0: .
L5@P0: Karnataka comprises 30 second-order administrative divisions, known as districts 2
L6@P0: . The
L7@P0: governor of Karnataka has issued a notification, which came into effect on 1st November 2014,
L8@P0: officially changing the spellings of the names of 12 cities in the state to reflect the names in the
L9@P0: local Kannada language. Several of these cities are the administrative seats of the districts of
L10@P0: the same name. While the official notification refers to the cities, it appears that the district
L11@P0: names are also changing3
L12@P0: .
L13@P0: These name changes are not yet reflected on all Indian government websites. The previous
L14@P0: spellings are still used in the list of districts on the Official Website of the Government of
L15@P0: Karnataka, but the new names are included on the National Informatics Centre (NIC) website4
L16@P0: and they can also be found on some district and city websites (see the table on the next page).
L17@P0: It is likely to be some time before all names on signs are updated to reflect the changes5 and
L18@P0: institutions such as Mysore University and Bangalore University are expected to retain their
L19@P0: existing names6
L20@P0: .
L21@P0: PCGN policy for India is to use the Roman-script geographical names found on official sources7
L22@P0: .
L23@P0: The new names listed on the next page should be used for the populated places and the
L24@P0: districts on all future UK government products or updates. In cases where the previous name is
L25@P0: well-known or differs significantly from the new name, e.g. Bengaluru (Bangalore), Mangaluru
L26@P0: (Mangalore), Vijayapura (Bijapur), Kalaburagi (Gulbarga) and Mysuru (Mysore), it may be
L27@P0: helpful to include the previous name in brackets. The bracketed names may become obsolete
L28@P0: in the future as the new spellings gain currency.
