Renaming of Cities

For changing the names of villages, towns/cities, railway stations etc., the proposals received from the respective State Government are considered in the Union Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) in consultation with the agencies concerned. Thereafter, if found appropriate, the MHA conveys its  ‘No Objection’ to the State Government concerned for issuing  required Gazette Notification.

The MHA has issued guidelines for changing names of cities/towns etc. If any proposal is received from any State fulfilling these guidelines, this Ministry accords its ‘No Objection’ to such change of name.

 

This was stated by the Minister of State for Home Affairs Shri Haribhai Parathibhai Chaudhary in a written reply to Shri Keshav Prasad Maurya in the Lok Sabha today.
