Renaming of Cities Print X Close Press Information Bureau Government of India Ministry of Home Affairs 16-December-2014 14:36 IST Renaming of Cities For
changing the names of villages, towns/cities, railway stations etc., the proposals
received from the respective State Government are considered in the Union Ministry
of Home Affairs (MHA) in consultation with the agencies concerned. Thereafter,
if found appropriate, the MHA conveys its  ‘No Objection’ to the State
Government concerned for issuing  required Gazette Notification. The MHA has given its ‘No Objection’ for
change of names of 12 cities recently in the State of Karnataka as per list
given below: Sl.No. Old
  name of the city /town New
  name of the city/town 1. BANGALORE BENGALURU 2. MANGALORE MANGALURU 3. BELLARY BALLARI 4. BIJAPUR VIJAYAPURA
  OR VIJAPURA 5. BELGAUM BELAGAVI 6. CHIKMAGALUR CHIKKAMAGALURU 7. GULBARGA KALABURAGI 8. MYSORE MYSURE 9. HOSPET HOSAPETE 10. SHIMOGA SHIVAMOGGA 11. HUBLI HUBBALLI 12. TUMKUR TUMAKURU The MHA has issued guidelines for
changing names of cities/towns etc. If any proposal is received from any State
fulfilling these guidelines, this Ministry accords its ‘No Objection’ to such
change of name. This was stated by the Minister of State for Home
Affairs Shri Haribhai Parathibhai Chaudhary in a written reply to Shri Keshav
Prasad Maurya in the Lok Sabha today. **** KSD/YKB/BK/RS
